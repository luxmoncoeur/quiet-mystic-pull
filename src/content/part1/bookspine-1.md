This book spine poem is all about the fine line between conviction and reality. The organization of the book titles leads the reader through thoughts about the nature of certainty, and the possibility of being wrong.

It opens with confidence: a *Decade of Decision*, a group that *Led the Way* and is *Having Our Say*. Then the tone turns. *Unforeseen Circumstances* break the plan, and the poem ends on a *Grave Mistake*, a reminder that being sure of something is not the same as being right.
