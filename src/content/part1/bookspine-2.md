By arranging these book titles, a poem about waiting for an answer has been made. Note the words used, which hint that only after being confronted with the truth will the subject of this poem know what they have so long searched for.

The poem begins at a *Moment of Truth*, then moves through fear, *The Terror That Comes in the Night*, and avoidance, *The Denial of Death*. Only when *We Came to the End* does the subject stop running and start *Asking About Life*, as if the answer was never in the ending, but in finally asking the question.
