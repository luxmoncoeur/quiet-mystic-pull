This version of John Everett Millais's *Ophelia* focuses on love, not suicide. I have replaced the scenery with myself and my favorite flowers. In addition, I have kept the same composition, Victorian clothing, the river from the original painting, as well as the artist's signature Pre-Raphaelite style. However, I have changed the water where the woman floats to a turbulent one to demonstrate that beneath the surface, everything is not as calm as it seems. The flowers represent someone overwhelmed by emotion and losing themselves in love.
